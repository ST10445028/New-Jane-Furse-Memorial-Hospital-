# New Jane Furse Memorial Hospital ICT Upgrade

## Project Overview

This project proposes an ICT infrastructure upgrade for New Jane Furse Memorial Hospital in Sekhukhune District, Limpopo, South Africa. It addresses outdated computers, limited printing capacity, unreliable patient-record storage, insufficient network connectivity, and cybersecurity risks.

The proposal aims to connect the hospital to the Sekhukhune District Municipality network over a dedicated fibre link and provide a more reliable, secure, and maintainable environment for clinical and administrative services. The work is presented as an academic project for **XITM6329: Work Integrated Learning**, prepared by **DITM Group 3**.

## Project Objectives

- Plan a dedicated fibre connection between the municipality and hospital, with Openserve identified as the proposed carrier.
- Modernise the hospital's network, structured cabling, wireless access, and end-user equipment.
- Improve secure storage, backup, and recovery of hospital information.
- Apply network segmentation, access controls, endpoint protection, and other cybersecurity measures.
- Train the hospital's three ICT support staff and provide documentation for operations and handover.
- Maintain continuity of essential hospital services during installation and recovery from outages.

## Proposed Solution

The submissions describe a hospital network connected to the municipal network by approximately **1 km of single-mode fibre**. The proposed hospital environment includes managed switching and Wi-Fi, firewalls, separate network segments for clinical and administrative services, secure supplier access, server and storage infrastructure, backup and disaster recovery, and ICT staff training.

The documents also outline operating procedures for hardware and software, network setup, incident response, and business continuity. These are proposals and planning materials, not evidence that the infrastructure has been installed or approved for operational use.

## Planning Figures

The submissions describe an estimated project budget of **R5,335,000** and a target delivery period of **12 months**. These are planning estimates, not approved funding or a committed delivery schedule.

Some figures differ between sections of the submitted documents. For example, printer quantities are variously described as **5** and **15**, and schedule sections describe a **12-month** target, an **8.5-month** activity plan, and a **16-week** high-level schedule. Reconcile these assumptions during requirements validation and baseline approval before procurement or implementation.
